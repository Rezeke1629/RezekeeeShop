Help & FAQ

At RezekeeeShop, we aim to make your online shopping experience smooth, fast, and secure with flexible payment options.

On this page

Payment methods Pricing Payment security FAQ

I. Accepted Payment Methods

  1. KHQR (Scan to Pay): Scan and pay using Bakong or any local mobile banking app across Cambodia.
  2. Wing Bank: Direct transfer or payment through the Wing Bank app.
  3. VISA Card: Secure payment using valid VISA Credit or Debit cards.
  4. Cash on Delivery (COD): Pay in cash upon delivery in designated locations.

II. Pricing & No Hidden Charges

  • Transparent Pricing: There are no hidden charges or surprise fees.
  • All-Inclusive Product Prices: The listed product price is the final item price.
  • Delivery Fees: Standard delivery charges may apply depending on your location.

III. Payment Security & Fraud Prevention

  • Secure Encryption: Card and online payments use high-level security protocols.
  • 3D Secure Verification: Card payments use 3D Secure verification from partner banks.
  • Transaction Safety: Suspicious payments may require identity verification before dispatch.

IV. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is it safe to use my card or bank details?

A: Yes. Payments use encrypted banking partners and secure gateways. We never store full card details or banking passwords.

Q: What currencies do you accept?

A: We accept US Dollars (USD) and Khmer Riels (KHR) through KHQR or cash delivery.
